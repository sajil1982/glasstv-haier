# Credits & third-party licenses

glasstv is a fork of **CleanYourTV** and stands on several open assets. All are
redistributable; this file records what they are and under which terms.

## Base project
- **CleanYourTV** — the launcher, debloat tooling, device examples and docs this
  project forked from. © 2026 Gathin, MIT License.
  https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv

## Visual design
- **CleanFrame** by Nathan Clark — the home-screen look glasstv's launcher adopts
  (photo background, frosted glass dock, white monochrome app marks, clock + date).
  MIT License. https://github.com/nthnclrk/CleanFrame
  The Prime Video, Apple TV and Settings marks come from CleanFrame's icon set.

  `launcher/ICONS-LICENSE-FLUENT-EMOJI.txt`.

Brand names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners; the monochrome
marks are provided only to identify the apps a user has installed.

## Wallpapers
- The paper-cut and weather-lake wallpaper sets are generated entirely from code
  (`tools/wallpaper-generators/`) and carry no third-party image.
- `photo_timeofday.py` grades a photo **you** supply into four times of day; no
  stock photo is bundled.
